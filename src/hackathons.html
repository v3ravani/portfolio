Why I Hack

For me, hackathons are the perfect place to test ideas, sharpen skills, and create real impact. Give me a problem, a team, and a deadline that's way too short, and I'm in. I enjoy brainstorming, building from scratch, debugging unexpected issues, and presenting solutions that can make a difference. Every hackathon teaches me something new and helps me grow as both an engineer and a teammate.

Winning ₹1,15,000 in combined prize money has been rewarding, but the experience is what keeps me coming back. I've met amazing people, learned from different perspectives, and discovered that some of the best ideas come under pressure. It's challenging, sometimes chaotic, and usually powered by coffee, but I wouldn't have it any other way.
